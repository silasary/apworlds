# NEO: The World Ends with You Setup Guide

## What Does Randomization do to this Game?

- Currently, only locations / items up to the end of Week 1 Day 4 are randomized.
- Story rewards, quest rewards and brand VIP levels are always randomized.
- The following locations / items can be randomized via options:
   - Shop goods
   - Dive rewards
   - Noise drops
   - Pig drops
   - Social Network Nodes
   - Skills
   - DLC items

## What is the Goal of NEO: The World Ends with You in Archipelago

The current goal is to reach Week 1 Day 5.

## What is Required to Play the Game in Archipelago?
The Steam version of NEO: The World Ends with You is required in order to play this game.

Additionally, you will have to install this [Client mod](https://github.com/Sjerver/NEO-Twewy-Archipelago-Mod/releases)
for the game.

## What Other Adjustments have been made to the Base Game?

1. Most menu options are unlocked after completing the battle tutorial.
2. Fast-forwarding through dialogue is unlocked from the start.
3. Completing a day counts as an Archipelago location that is always randomized.
4. In order to unlock a day you need at least as many Secret Reports as the days number - 1. Example: Week 1 Day 2 needs
   1 Secret Report, Week 1 Day 5 needs 4, Week 3 Day 4 would need 17 Secret Reports etc.
5. Completing a day without having unlocked the next one, repeats the day from the beginning.
6. While repeating a day you can press a configured button to skip to the end of the day. The default key is F5 and can be changed in the config file.
7. The 1 Yen Pin has been replaced by the Archipelago Pin representing items from other worlds. This pin is worth 1000
   Yen.
8. Drop rate increasing threads have a maximum style requirement of 50.
9. Soundsurf can be unlocked at the start via an optional setting.
10. VIP levels are always randomized. Their rewards can be obtained by entering a shop of the corresponding brand.
11. Unlocking a restaurant owners SNS node only requires eating at the restaurant 1 time instead of 3.
12. Social Network nodes whose unlocks are randomized, are visible from the start.
13. Purchasing skills / skill locations from the Social Network, does not consume FP.
14. You can view your currently owned skills in the "Thoughts" menu under the "Mental Notes" tab at the top of the
    current days list.
15. The current objective text is replaced with the latest message received from Archipelago.
16. The connection status is shown next to the current difficulty.
