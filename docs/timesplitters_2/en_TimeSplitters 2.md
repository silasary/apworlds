# TimeSplitters 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

All 10 story levels (or 10 Progressive Story Levels, which open them in order), all 45 Arcade League missions and 21 Challenges, all 126 characters, 9 cheats,
the 5 Silly Hats, 16 arcade maps (Hangar, Scrapyard and Training Ground also unlock their Assault versions), 16
arcade game modes and 26 arcade weapons are locked until you receive them from the multiworld. A group opens as soon
as you have one of its missions, and only the missions you have are playable in it. You start
with one mission, chosen by the Starting Item option, one random character and one random
weapon. You start with no maps or game modes.

Nothing unlocks the normal way any more: completing a story level does not open the next one,
and earning a trophy does not unlock the next mission, a character or a cheat.

Characters, cheats, hats, maps, modes and weapons are never needed to reach a check (Arcade League and
Challenge missions use their own maps and modes). The Silly Hats cheat itself is
always available, as in the normal game; the hats it offers are the items. Infinite Ammo is not
in the game: it is not an item and cannot be earned. Weapons are the ones offered when building
a custom weapon set in Arcade Custom; owning a weapon also unlocks its (x2) version. Unarmed is
always available.

Bonus items (each Off, Start With One or Shuffle All):

* Progressive Behead The Undead Score Multiplier (three): every kill in Fight Off The Living
  Dead, Day of the Dammed and Sergio's Last Stand scores x2, x3, then x4 points (the combo bonus
  grows with it).

* Progressive Starting Armour (four): you start each life with 25%, 50%, 75%, then a full bar
  of armour: when a mission starts, after a restart, after retrying a Story level you died in
  and when you respawn. It applies in Story, every Arcade League mission, Behead The Undead,
  Banana Chomp and TimeSplitters 'Story' Classic, and never lowers armour you already have.

* Weapon buffs ("Include Weapon Buffs", on by default): "Weapon Buff - <weapon> - +25% Clip
  Size" and "... +25% Reserve Ammo" items fill the locations left over after every other item.
  Each copy (up to three) adds 25% of the normal value. A bigger clip shows on the next reload.
  The client window has an "Inventory" tab: the shop's points and what its rows show now (buy
  in the game's own shop menu), then the damage resistances, weapon buffs, score multipliers and
  starting armour you have.

* Damage resistances ("Include Damage Resistances", on by default): Progressive Bullet,
  Explosive, Fire, Plasma and Melee Damage Resistance, six of each. Every copy takes 10% off
  that kind of damage you take, up to 60%; enemies are not affected. Bullet covers bullets,
  shotgun pellets and crossbow bolts; Explosive covers rockets, grenades, mines, TNT and every
  explosion's blast; Fire covers the Flamethrower, burning and the Fire Extinguisher; Plasma
  covers the Lasergun, Plasma Autorifle, Scifi Handgun and ElectroTool; Melee covers punches
  and the Brick. They fill the leftover locations together with the weapon buffs, each copy
  twice as likely to be picked as a weapon buff, so most games get most of them. Needs
  Dolphin's cheat codes to be off.

* Cheat traps ("Include Cheat Traps", off by default): Big Heads, Small Heads, Big Hands, Fat
  Characters, Cardboard Characters, Rotating Heads, All Characters Cloaked and Slow Motion
  Deaths traps, at most three of each. A trap waits while you are in the menus and switches its
  cheat on as soon as you are playing, for "Cheat Trap Duration" seconds of play (10-120,
  default 30). Cheats you switched on yourself are left alone. Traps take the place of "Nothing"
  items first, and are shuffled in with the weapon buffs and resistances when there are none left.

* Easier Top Shot ("Easier Top Shot", on by default): Top Shot's Platinum needs 1st place within
  2:00 instead of 1:10. Silver (4:00) and Gold (3:00) stay the same.

* Death Link ("Death Link", off by default): failing a Story level, Arcade League mission or
  Challenge (dying, a failed objective, or finishing below the trophy you need) sends a death to
  everyone else using Death Link. A death you receive fails the story level you are playing, or
  kills you in an Arcade League mission or Challenge (the game then takes a life, respawns you or
  fails the mission, as for any other death); in the menus it does nothing. A death that arrives during a story cutscene waits until you can move.
  In Arcade League missions where you respawn, a death is sent every few deaths instead: "Death
  Link Amnesty" (1 to 5, default 3) sets how many deaths in one mission send one (1 = every
  death). The count starts again with every mission, including a restart. Dolphin's cheats must
  be off to receive deaths.

A multiplier received during a mission counts from the next point you score. If you had already
played one of these missions before the client connected, the multipliers start working once
you go back through the mission select screen. Dolphin's cheats must be off.

Only the North American GameCube version (game ID `GTSE4F`) is supported.

## What are the checks?

* Trophies: a Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum trophy on a mission sends a check for that trophy
  and every lower one.
  Exclude All Platinum Trophies, or Exclude Specific Platinum Trophies with a list of mission
  names (for example ["Dam Bursters"]), keeps progression items off those Platinum checks.
* Story levels: completing a level on Easy, Normal and Hard are three checks (finishing on a
  higher difficulty also counts the lower ones, as in the normal game). Single player only.
* Story objectives: each objective is one check, on any difficulty where it appears, at the
  moment the game marks it complete. Optional objectives count the same as main ones (for
  example "Burn All Evidence in the Filing Cabinets", optional on Easy and Normal, main on Hard).
  69 in all.
  "Skip Easy Mode Exclusive Story Objectives" (off by default, under Difficulty Settings): 2019
  NeoTokyo's "Eliminate the Gang Leader before She Escapes" only exists on Easy; with this on, it
  is checked when you complete 2019 NeoTokyo on Normal or Hard instead.
* Temporal Uplink cartridges: picking up the Anaconda cartridge in 1990 Siberia, the RetroRacer
  cartridge in 2315 Robot Factory (Hard only; excluded with Exclude Hard Story) and the
  AstroLander cartridge in 2019 NeoTokyo. The games themselves are unlocked by the items
  "Temporal Uplink - Anaconda / RetroRacer / AstroLander Cartridge": the Temporal Uplink's
  Select Game menu only offers the games whose items you have.
* Temporal Uplink games (all off by default, under "Temporal Uplink Settings"):
  * Anaconda: "Temporal Uplink - Anaconda - 500 Points" and so on, one per 500 points scored in
    a single game, as many as "How many Anaconda Checks?" says (0-20, up to 10,000 points).
  * RetroRacer: "Temporal Uplink - RetroRacer - Lap 1" to "Lap 5" and "Completed" (the last lap
    and Completed are reached together, when the race ends).
  * AstroLander: "Temporal Uplink - AstroLander - Level 1 Completed" and so on, as many as
    "How many AstroLander Checks?" says (0-11).
  Each game needs its cartridge item and at least one story level, as they can only be played
  from the Temporal Uplink during Story.
* Milestones (each kind can be switched off in the options): "AL - Beginners Series - Group
  Completed" and the like for a Bronze or better on every mission of a group, "Amateur /
  Honorary / Elite League Completed" for every mission of a league, and "All Challenges
  Completed".
* Archipelago Shop ("Include Archipelago Shop", on by default): "Archipelago Shop Item 1" and
  so on, bought with points in the shop (see below). "How Many Shop Locations": 13 (default),
  26, 39, 52 or 65.

## What is the goal?

Chosen with the Goal option:

* 2401 Space Station (default): complete 2401 Space Station on any difficulty. It opens once you
  have it AND meet the extra requirements under "Goal Options - 2401 Space Station": a number of
  other story levels completed (0 to 9) and a number of Arcade League missions or Challenges
  beaten with at least a chosen trophy grade (0 to 66). Completing it is the goal, not a check.
* Trophy Hunt: earn the chosen grade of trophy or better on all Arcade League missions, all
  Challenges, or all 66 ("Goal Options - Trophy Hunt"). With this goal, completing 2401 Space
  Station on each difficulty is three ordinary checks, and Exclude Story Mode can keep anything
  required to win out of Story Mode locations.

## Archipelago Shop
With "Include Archipelago Shop" on, Options > View Cutscenes becomes the shop: the main menu's
Options reads "Options & Shop" and View Cutscenes reads "Archipelago Shop". Its title shows your
points, and its 13 rows each show a shop location's item, whose it is and its price, e.g.
"Hookshot (Link) - 60 Points". A buys it (sending that location's check) if you have the
points; the row then reads "Purchased". Cutscenes can't be watched from that menu.
* Points: every trophy (Bronze 10, Silver 20, Gold 30, Platinum 40; a better trophy counts the
  ones below it, so a first-try Platinum is 100), every story level finished (Easy 15, Normal 35,
  Hard 50; finishing on Hard counts all three), every kill (1, in any mode) and the Bronze,
  Silver, Gold and Platinum Trophy items (10 / 20 / 30 / 40 points; no trophy in the game).
  Kills and points spent are kept on the server, so they are never lost.
* Prices: 1-15 points for filler and traps, 5-75 for useful items, 15-200 for progression, and
  85-200 for a story level (this game's or TimeSplitters: Future Perfect's).
* Restocks: with more than 13 shop locations, each further 13 needs a Progressive Shop Restock
  item (one for 26, up to four for 65). A restock gives every row its next item: a row already
  "Purchased" shows it the next time you open the shop, and a row not yet bought shows it as
  soon as you buy it.
* TimeSplitters 2 and TimeSplitters: Future Perfect items are named as on the award screen: a
  mission or story level by its own name (e.g. "Fight Off The Living Dead", "Commuting Will Kill
  You"), a weapon buff as "Pistols +25% Reserve Ammo". The award screen names other players'
  items of those two games the same way.
* Hints ("Shop Hints": None, All, or Progression Only by default): when you open the shop, the
  items in its 13 rows are hinted to their owners; a row's next item is hinted when buying brings
  it up. Nothing is hinted before the shop is opened. With Progression Only, only progression
  items are hinted.
* Items bought in the shop are not listed on the award screen (not even characters, maps and
  the like that the game lists itself).
* Trophy items take the place of every "Nothing" and also join the weapon buffs and resistances
  for the leftover locations, now and then.
* Needs Dolphin's cheat codes to be off.

## In-game announcements
While you play, the client shows Archipelago news in the game's own text at the bottom of the
screen: items you receive ("Received ... from ..."), items you send ("Sent ... to ...") and your own
items you find ("Found ..."). Death Links you receive, and a trap's name when it starts ("BIG HEADS
TRAP"), show in large text in the middle. This works in
Story, Arcade League and Challenges; messages wait while a story cutscene plays, and messages that
arrive while you are in the menus are not shown.

Type `/announce` in the client to see the settings, `/announce off` or `/announce on` to switch them
all, or for example `/announce sent off`, `/announce traps off` or `/announce filler off` to hide one kind. Your choice is
remembered. Announcements need Dolphin's cheat codes to be off.

## Award screen
After a mission, the "New Feature Unlocked" screen also lists what you found at the locations you
checked, and what other players sent you, since the last time it was shown:
1. your story levels, arcade missions, challenges and Temporal Uplink cartridges ("New Story Level
   available!", "New Mission available!", "New Challenge available!", "New Cartridge available!"),
2. your arcade maps, characters, hats, game modes and cheats (listed by the game itself, as usual;
   its "New Challenge Set available!" lines are left out, as the challenges are listed above),
3. your arcade weapons ("New Weapon available!"), then score multipliers ("Score Multiplier
   found!", e.g. "Behead The Undead x3"), then starting armour ("Archipelago Item found!", e.g.
   "50% Starting Armour"), then damage resistances ("New Resistance found!", e.g. "Fire Damage
   20%"), then weapon buffs ("Clip Size Buff found!" or "Reserve Ammo Buff found!", e.g.
   "Tommy Gun +50%"). Progressive items show what you have once that copy arrives;
4. items for other players ("<player>'s Item!"): progression items first, then useful, traps and
   filler.
Every heading ends in "!", including the game's own "New Weapon / Assault Level / Arcade League
available!". Your own cheat traps are not listed. The mission or story level you have just played is not listed, even if its item arrived while you
were in the menus. The screen holds 10 entries. After an Arcade League mission the
screen also appears when only Archipelago items were found; otherwise it only appears
when the game has something of its own to show, and the Archipelago items join it then. This needs
Dolphin's cheat codes to be off.
