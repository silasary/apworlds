# SteamWorld Dig 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every reward in the game becomes a location, and the tool upgrades, blueprints, artifacts and cogs are shuffled into the
item pool. Shops sell items from the multiworld for in-game money, so digging and selling ore still matter.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Rosie. The Reactor door requires the Device items. By default that means all 4, or 3 with `Generators Required` set to Vanilla.

## What other changes are made to the game?

- The opening cutscene is skipped
- Items you receive wait until you've fallen into the Temple, so nothing can skip the earthquake
- The Reactor door waits for the required Device items
  - Breaking a generator is a location and no longer opens the door
- Vectron is skipped
  - A bomb through the teleporter room's ceiling opens the way to the Jet Engine podium
- Quests can no longer lock you out
  - Fen's quests are side quests
- El Machino's welcome speech always plays when walking into the trigger on the left
- Rosie's Storage opens once its quest is done (in case you skipped Rosie's first interaction)
- Ignition Axe damage against the Priest Boss (guarding the Ignition Axe podium) is normalized

## What does another world's item look like in SteamWorld Dig 2?

Cog containers look the same as usual, and an artifact's spot holds what looks like an Upgrade Cog. A banner tells you what you found in these and on podiums once you take it. Shop slots show the Archipelago logo with the item and who it's for unless `Show Shop Items` is off.

## What if I get stuck?

You might find yourself stuck in a cave after using a podium, and Self-Destruct sends you back to the podium. Instead, return to the main menu and reload the save, which will send you back to the entrance.
