# The Forest's End

## What does randomization do to this game?

All abilities (optionally including the basic hover and melee attack), health upgrades, hover upgrades, and lore
entries are shuffled into the item pool.

## What other changes are made to the game?

The cutscene where a path gets blocked early on in the giant tree does not play, allowing easier backtracking.
Restarting from the main menu will also retain progress from items previously obtained.
