# DOOM Eternal

Collect campaign rewards to send items across your Archipelago room. Other
players can send you weapons, equipment, abilities and upgrades. Solo rooms
shuffle those items within your own campaign.

## Choose your campaign

Use the [player options page](../player-options) or **CREATE PLAYER** in the
DOOM Eternal launcher before generation. Choose missions from the base game
and The Ancient Gods, a starting weapon, and a victory goal.

**Random Mission Order** shuffles mission progression. **Mission Access as Items**
lets you unlock missions by finding their Access items. **Vanilla Order** follows
their original order. Your **Goal** can require the Unmaykr, the Icon of Sin,
the Dark Lord or the Full Saga; **Additional Victory Requirements** adds tasks.

Your starting weapon, explicit starting inventory, and nonrandomized Dash and
Chainsaw follow your player options. Combat equipment comes from checks.
Progressive enemizer campaigns can start gradually in Hell on Earth or Exultia:
collect weapons in the Fortress and the opening areas before tackling the harder
encounters later in the mission. Other starting pools may need explicit starting
equipment; generation rejects a start that cannot supply its required items.

## Find rewards

Pickups, secrets, combat encounters, mission challenges, weapon masteries and
mission completion can send checks to the server. Their rewards may belong to
any player. Received consumables restore resources; traps can spawn enemies
or drain supplies.

**Enemy Randomizer** changes the enemies you fight. Choose a preset and adjust
its enemy weights, count and attributes. **Killsanity** adds rewards at kill-counter
targets, including observed deaths during mission replays. Auto estimates how
many rewards to add; a fixed reward count can require replays.

**DeathLink** shares deaths with enabled players. Soft lets Extra Lives or Saving
Throw protect you; Hardcore causes death and a checkpoint reload.

## Install and play

Read the [setup guide](setup/en) for requirements, installation and connection.
Use the matching standalone DOOM Eternal launcher to play. Install this APWorld
in Archipelago when generating a room.

This is a beta. The candidate has offline Windows validation; complete gameplay
and real Linux/Proton tests remain pending. Game updates may need a matching
runtime update. Keep backups when testing a candidate.
