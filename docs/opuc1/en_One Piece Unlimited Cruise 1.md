# One Piece Unlimited Cruise 1

## What is randomized?

This world covers the **Thousand Sunny, Island 1 (Wooded Isle), Island 2 (Gorge Isle), Island 3 (Iceberg Isle)
and the final island (Central Isle)** of One Piece Unlimited Cruise Part 1 (Wii, PAL). Island 4 is planned.
Every island is in every seed, whatever the goal.

**Locations** (about 440 with the default options):
- Move level-ups (levels 2-5 of every move) and, optionally, super and co-op move level-ups
- Story events, ambushes and bosses, including the secret boss
- Treasure chests and treasure map dig spots
- Stone Monuments
- Gabri Shrines and Build Sites
- The first time you craft each Development, Cooking and Compounding recipe
- Fishing and creature catching (optional)

**Items:**
- The Straw Hats (you start with one; the others are items)
- Moves, co-op moves and abilities (including the Art of Development / Cooking / Compounding)
- Tools and key items: Pickaxe, Bug-Catcher Net, Vacuum Cleaner, Fishing Rod and its upgrades, and others
- Usopp's Workshop, Sanji's Kitchen and Chopper's Pharmacy
- The islands: "Gorge Isle, The Cliff Ruins", "Iceberg Isle, Unexplored Ice Region" and "Central Isle, New
  Beginnings" (the Wooded Isle is always open)
- Gabri levels, shrine and build site access, "The Final Door", treasure map fragments
- Filler: food, medicine (also in packages of 3 and 5), Kuwagata and Orbs

**Goal** (option): defeat "God" Eneru, Gecko Moria, Aokiji, or the Doom Guardian on Central Isle (default; needs
"The Final Door" and all 13 Gabri levels). With any other goal the Doom Guardian fight is left out.

## What does another world's item look like?

Chests and recipes that hold an Archipelago location give an **Archipelago Item**. Recipe descriptions say whose
item the recipe holds (unless the "Crafting Recipes Spoil Locations" option is off).
