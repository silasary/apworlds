# Supermarket Simulator

## What does randomization do to this game?

The following elements are randomized and locked behind Archipelago items:

- **Product Licenses:** Access to buy and sell specific item categories in your store.
- **Store Sections:** Physical expansion rooms of your supermarket.
- **Furniture:** All Furnitures like Shelves, fridges, freezers, and checkout counters (if Furniture Locks is enabled).
- **Staff:** Cashiers, restockers, janitors, security guards, and customer helpers.
- **Storage Room Upgrades:** Expanding your storage room.
- **Vehicles:** Personal transport for faster restocking.
- **Loan Authorizations:** Credit line unlock permits (if Loan Locks is enabled).

## What is the goal when randomized?

You can choose between three goals in your settings:

- **Level:** Reach the configured maximum store level.
- **Days:** Complete the configured number of operating days.
- **All Licenses:** Collect every product license available in the item pool (base game + active DLCs).

## What counts as a location check?

Completing store days, reaching store level milestones (starting at Level 2), purchasing storage room upgrades, buying section expansions, purchasing received product licenses at the store computer (`Purchase License X`), completing customer checkouts (`Customer Checkout X`), and reaching money milestones (if enabled) all act as **checks** and send items to other players in the multiworld.

## What items can I receive?

In addition to the locked game elements above, you may also receive:

- **Money Boost:** A cash bonus deposited directly into your store account.
- **XP Boost:** Bonus store experience to accelerate level progression.
- **Black Friday Event:** A surprise customer rush floods your store.

## What are traps?

If traps are enabled, you may receive the following negative events:

- **Tax Audit:** A fine deducted from your bank account.
- **Dust Storm:** Your store window is instantly covered in dust.
- **Power Outage:** The store lights go out for a period of time.
- **Trash Flood:** A wave of garbage spawns on your store floor.
- **Expired Products:** Products on a random display shelf are instantly removed.
- **Robbery:** A group of shoplifters spawns.

## Balance & Gameplay Options

You can configure several options in your settings to customize and balance your playthrough:

- **Exclude Licenses:** Exclude specific product licenses by name from the "All Licenses" victory goal. These licenses will still be in the item pool as useful items but are not required for victory.
- **Customer Checkout Locations & Chance:** Configure how many customer checkout checks exist in the multiworld (0–10000, default 100) and the percentage chance per checked-out customer to trigger a check (1–100%, default 5%).
- **Checkout Income Multiplier:** Multiply checkout payouts received at cash registers/self-checkouts (from 10% to 1000%). Also mutliplies money boosts.
- **Starting Cash:** Customize how much money you start with in a new save game (from $10 to $10,000).
- **Free Customizables:** Toggle to make all visual shop upgrades (wall paint, floor tiles, store rename, door placement, entrance variants) cost $0 since they do not affect progression.

## DLC Compatibility

The following DLCs are fully supported and can be individually enabled via your settings:

- Bakery DLC
- Ice Cream DLC
- Vending Machine DLC
- Electronics DLC
- Hardware DLC
- Clothing DLC
- Essentials DLC

If a DLC is disabled in your settings, all associated product licenses, items, and features will remain locked in-game.
