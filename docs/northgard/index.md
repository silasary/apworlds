# Northgard

Northgard is a Viking-themed strategy game. This randomizer covers Conquest mode: complete your Conquest map, solo or in co-op, by winning Chapter 7. Progress through the branching tree of Chapters is gated by items received from the multiworld; completing a Chapter&#x27;s battle sends its location checks.
