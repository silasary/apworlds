# Death's Door

## What does randomization do to this game?
Items you would normally pick up, such as weapons, spells, Shiny Things, Soul Orbs, Life Seeds, Vitality and Magic Shards, Ancient Tablets, levers, doors and Lost Crows, are shuffled across your world and the rest of the multiworld. Shop upgrades, planting Life Seeds and the entrances between areas can be randomized too.

## What is the goal?
Depending on your options: defeat the Lord of Doors, get the True Ending with all 7 Ancient Tablets of Knowledge, open the Green Tablet door by planting Life Seeds, or any of these.
