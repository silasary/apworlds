# Pokeclicker Randomizer Setup Guide

## Required Software

- [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases/latest)
- [The Pokeclicker apworld](https://github.com/Hallstead/AP-Pokeclicker/releases), 
  if not bundled with your version of Archipelago

## How to play

First, you need a room to connect to. For this, you or someone you know has to generate a game.  
This will not be explained here,
but you can check the [Archipelago Setup Guide](/tutorial/Archipelago/setup_en#generating-a-game).

You also need to have [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases/latest) installed
and the [The Pokeclicker apworld](https://github.com/Hallstead/AP-Pokeclicker/releases) installed into Archipelago.

From here, connecting to your Pokeclicker slot is easy.

### Connecting to the slot

Open the [AP Pokeclicker Client](https://hallstead.github.io/AP-Pokeclicker/) in your browser of choice. 
Enter the server address, port number, player/slot name, and password (if one exists) and click "Connect (Archipelago)".  
You should now be connected and able to play Pokeclicker.

## Feeling Stuck?

Suntimes it can be tricky to know what you can or cannot do in a given moment or what the game expects you to be aiming for next.
There is a tracker that lets you see what locations are logically available at that moment.

1. Download [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases/latest)
2. Put the tracker.apworld file into the "custom_worlds" directory
3. Open the Archipelago Launcher, and open the "Universal Tracker".
4. Enter the Archipelago server address:port (the one you connected your client to), click "Connect" and follow the prompts to enter your slot name and password.
5. Once connected, click on the "Tracker Page" tab to see what locations are in logic.
