## What is AP Pokeclicker?

Pokéclicker is a free, fan-made incremental game based on the Pokémon series. Instead of playing through turn-based battles
like the mainline games, your Pokémon automatically battle wild Pokémon while you unlock new routes, challenge Gym Leaders,
explore dungeons, catch Pokémon, and complete the Pokédex. The game follows the progression of the official Pokémon games but
focuses on collection, resource management, and long-term progression.

This Archipelago implementation modifies that progression by randomizing where key rewards are found. Instead of receiving
important items and Pokémon through their normal locations, they are shuffled throughout the game as Archipelago checks.
Progression items, key items, and even obtained Pokémon may be found in unexpected places, requiring you to complete different
objectives before you can continue.
