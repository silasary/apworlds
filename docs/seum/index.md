# SEUM Speedrunners from Hell

SEUM: Speedrunners from Hell is a first-person speedrunning platformer in which
Marty blasts his way through the floors of Hell to get his soul back.
Every level unlock is an item and every completed level is a check.
Beat F9-1 S(A)TAN, the final boss, to win.
