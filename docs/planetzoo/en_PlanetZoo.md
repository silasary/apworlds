## How to Randomize a Zoo? Don't we do this anyway?

This Planet Zoo Randomizer creates a more linear path of completing the game as usual, defines an arbituary goal (currently only breeding Giant Pandas), and allows you to play in Archipelago runs with your friends (using this game of course). 

Of course, it can't be too easy, and is definitely more tedious by making research centres and workshops unlockables, as well as water tools, barriers, the conservation program. (Spoiler: Giant Pandas require at least level 5 barriers, so get grinding!)

Planet Zoo can only be played alone, and this implementation only makes you feel less alone while playing <3

**Note! Only base game is currently supported**

## Locations checks and Items

Location checks are all researchables, plus first aquisition, breeding and conservation releases of each animal. Zoo rating and Guest amounts are also checks. This means, you have alot of checks, so use your hints wisely.

Items are on the easier side, being either money, conservation points, or more useful things like Blueprints (from the base game, we are not that fancy), different shops for your guests, and most importantly, enrichment items for your animals.

## Other Game Modifications

The spawn rates in the Market have been modified so you don't sit there waiting for two in-game years to pass before you get your pandas. (Been there, done that) 

### Randomization Options

Since it seems standard (who am I kidding, Overcooked does it so fine) to list options, here they are

- *Starting Money*
- *Number of Starting Species*
- *Starting Barrier Level*
- *Amounts of Filler items*
    - Seperate for Cash and Conservation Points
    - Amount shown is what will double for the larger amount, and half for the small
    - If you want an easy time, make it a higher number, you'll never have to dream of being poor again.
    - Contrary to phrasing, does not affect how many of these items will be received. It's a filler!
