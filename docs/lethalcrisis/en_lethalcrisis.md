# Lethal Crisis

## Summary

A sidescrolling action game by Daisessen, featuring 25 stages with 5 unique objectives each.

## Where is the settings page?

The [player settings page for this game](../player-settings) contains all the options you need to configure and
export a config file.

## What is the objective of the randomizer?

The objective is to get Stage 17B's D rank, achieving the game's true ending.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

All stage rank rewards. Optionally, the four applications Clarino starts with can also be shuffled with other applications
in their categories.

## What does another world's item look like in Lethal Crisis?

Items from other games will appear in the rank unlock screen with the Archipelago icon and the name "AP Item". They do not appear in your inventory afterwards.

## Are there any other changes made?

- All ranks are revealed as soon as their corresponding stages are.
- The Stage 7B and Stage 10B unlocks are still tracked as individual items, but functionally act as progressive unlocks: once Stage 16's D rank is achieved, having one unlock item will reveal Stage 7B, and the second will reveal 10B.
- In the current release, revealing 10B reveals all of the B stages through 17B, which _somewhat_ trivializes the endgame. This is on the list for fixing, but it is what it is for now.
