# Children of Morta 

Children of Morta is a story-driven action RPG game about an extraordinary family of heroes. Lead the Bergsons, with all their flaws and virtues, against the forthcoming Corruption. Will you be able to sacrifice everything to save the ones you care for? (via [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/330020/Children_of_Morta/))

## What does randomization do to this game?

It allows the endless mode (Zyklus/Family trials) to be played with archipelago multiworld. It randomizes:
- playable characters
- divine relics
- talents

## What is the goal of Children of Morta when randomized?

In endless mode (currently only one) it's to defeat end boss:
- once with every character
- set amount of times

## Which items can be in another player's world?
All items that can be randomized.

## What does another world's item look like in Children of Morta?
Normal divine relics and talents are replaced with multiworld items. Each one tells what it gives and to who.
