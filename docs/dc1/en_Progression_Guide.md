# What is needed to progress to the next area?
- DBC Rear, Matataki Village and WOF Front requires finishing Progressive Players House, for a total of 7 Progressive Char Building.  Additionally, Gaffer's Buggy and Pike are logically required, but can be skipped.  UT does not currently account for needing these.
- WOF Rear, Queens and SW Front requires finishing Progressive Cacao's House, for a total of 14 Progressive Char Building as well as 5 Matataki River items
- SW Rear, Muska Lacka and SMT Front requires finishing King's Hideout, for a total of 21 Progressive Char Building
- SMT Rear, Yellow Drops, Factory and full MS (MS Rear doesnt have anything to unlock, only recruiting Osmond from floor 8) requires finishing 3 Sisters House, for a total of 28 Progressive Char Building
- GoT and Dark Heaven Castle need one finished of either HGR or HGL parts, and one finished of TIR or TIL parts in Yellow Drops Factory

Additionally, if miracle chests are shuffled accessing each character (excluding Xiao) and the final dungeon logically requires an amount of fruits of eden and defense upgrades but this is not reflected in trackers currently.

# Miracle Chests
- Chests appear based on progression in the local dungeon and can require specific georama.  If a chest is available according to a tracker, you either need to enter the dungeon or possibly `open_dungeon` is false and you need to make more progress in the dungeon.
- Access to the chests inside of Joker's House requires completing Joker's House and the Sheriff's Office, viewing the scene from the Sheriff before you can enter the house.

# Boss fight access
- Boss 1 Dran: Recruit Xiao.  Horned Key is needed.  If miracle chests are shuffled, this will be an item in the item pool.  Otherwise it is vanilla from completing Dran's Windmill and viewing the cutscene.
- Boss 2 Utan: Recruit Goro.  Completed Mushroom House building to get the Moon Grass Seed.  Also requires a sundew, either from the item pool with chests shuffled, a chest in the Mushroom House without, or random loot from the Wise Owl Forest.  If chests are shuffled, the one in the item pool is logically expected.
- Boss 3 Saia: Recruit Ruby.  Complete Cathedral + Divining House and view their cutscenes.  Enter the beach shop for the music box key.
- Boss 4 King's Curse: Recruit Ungaga.  Complete Chief's House, Zabo's House, and Enga's House.  View scene from Chief's house then visit the other 2 for the key items.
- Boss 5 Minotaur Joe: Recruit Osmond.  Complete Parts HD and view cutscene for key item.
- Boss 6 Dark Genie: Have access to Gallery of Time.  Complete X number of Seda's memory georama, where X is the `memory_count` yaml option.
