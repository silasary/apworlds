# Saints Row 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Respect to start missions alongside the unlocks to activities, collectibles and diversions have been randomized.

## What is the goal?

The goal is to complete all gang arcs you have enabled in your options.

## What is a "check" in Saints Row 2?

Missions, activity levels, Chop Shop vehicles, Hitman targets, CDs, Tags, Stunt Jumps.

## What "items" can you unlock in Saints Row 2?

Respect and activity/collectible/diversion unlocks are your main progression items. Other items are vehicles and weapons, alongside pure filler like clothes and money.
