# Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver

## Requirements for Different Events
* To enter the Bell Tower or the deepest basement of Whirl Islands, the rainbow wing or silver wing, respectively, are the only requirements.
* To encounter Ho-Oh in Pokémon HeartGold, the only requirement is the Clear Bell. Similarly,
  Lugia only requires the Tidal Bell in Pokémon SoulSilver.
* The team rocket takeover of the radio tower is triggered by acquiring the Fog Badge, Mineral Badge, Storm Badge, and Glacier Badge.
* The Master Ball gift from Prof. Elm is triggered by acquiring all eight Johto region gym badges, having completed the event in Burned Tower, and having defeated the Team Rocket grunt in the Ecruteak Dance Theater.
* The east exit of New Bark Town is unlocked by encountering the main legendary Pokémon of the game (Ho-Oh for HeartGold, Lugia for SoulSilver).
* The magnet train only requires the Pass item.
* The S.S. Anne only requires the S.S. Ticket item
* The northeast room in Ruins of Alph only requires the Rainbow Wing.
