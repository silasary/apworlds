# Wedding Witch

## What does randomization do to this game?

The witch's meta-upgrade shop stops selling upgrades. All 73 of its purchasable levels
become Archipelago items, so the only permanent power the witch has is what the
multiworld sends her; the shop's shelves are restocked with Archipelago checks, which
is what Love Coins buy instead. Your upgrade levels still show in the panel below the
stock, unbuyable.

Which difficulties you can play is also the multiworld's to decide, rather than the
game's usual "clear the last one to open the next".

## What is the goal?

Clear the Nightmare Wedding boss in a number of different witch forms — six by
default, settable from 1 to 6 with Goal Forms. The forms are Big Breasts, Small
Breasts, Corruption, Beast, Muscle and Hips, and the one you finish in is decided by
which potion type you levelled, so each is a separate winning run. Winning as the
untransformed witch never counts.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Locations are:

- **Potions** — drinking one of the three potions offered at the end of a stage. Each
  slot is its own check, so a stage pays out three across as many visits: 54 in all,
  over 5 stages on Normal, 6 on Hard and 7 on Nightmare. Turn off Potion Slot Locations
  for one check per screen instead, and 18 in all.
- **Bosses** — clearing a difficulty's boss. Nightmare splits this six ways, one per
  witch form; Normal and Hard just want it dead in any form, because their shorter runs
  rarely offer enough of one potion type to transform at all. 8 checks.
- **Shop** — buying a shelf in the witch's shop. Shop Locations of them, 20 by
  default.

A default seed is 82 locations: 54 potions, 8 boss clears and 20 shelves.

Collecting Nightmare's six boss forms already takes six runs of it, and six visits to a
stage more than cover its three potion slots — so on the difficulty that matters most,
the slots cost you very little beyond what the forms already ask.

Clearing a stage is not a check in itself. The potion screen it leads to is, so a
cleared stage already pays out once — counting the clear as well would have doubled
every run.

Items are the 17 shop upgrades, each named after the thing itself — a copy of
Witch's Hat raises its level, and it has five. Witch's Bag and Phantasm Mirror have a
single level each. Progressive Difficulty opens Hard and then Nightmare, replacing the
game's own "clear the last one first" unlock.

Whatever is left over is a wedding gift, prefixed `Gift:` — the witch is extorting
lingerie out of the devils, and a gift is worth exactly as much as that suggests. A
default seed has room for 17 of them.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

All of them.

## What does another world's item look like in Wedding Witch?

Nothing appears in the world itself — checks fire the moment you clear a stage, win
as a form, or complete an achievement, and the item goes straight to whoever owns
it.

## Notes

Your upgrade levels, coins and lifetime stats are kept per room. Connecting neither
reads nor writes your normal save.
