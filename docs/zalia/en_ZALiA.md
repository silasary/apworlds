# ZALiA (Zelda Again: Link is Adventuresome)

ZALiA is a standalonegame with a built-in Archipelago client. It connects directly to an Archipelago room, applies the slot's generated settings to a new save, sends checks, receives items, restores server-confirmed checks after reconnecting, and displays Archipelago messages and hints in-game.

The current client requires a seed generated by the current ZALiA APWorld.
Unsupported older slots are rejected with an explanatory message.

## Features

- Direct Archipelago connection; no separate client required
- In-game Archipelago messages, item classifications, and check markers
- Item-location hint givers and optional Zelda hints
- Shuffled towns, palaces, bosses, spells, keys, and other slot settings applied from slot data
- Optional boss-item and individual Kakusu checks (golden slimes)
- DeathLink
- Universal Tracker regeneration data and labels for relocated towns and palaces

## Game Options

### Item Options

- **randomize_item_locations** — Shuffle main items, quest items, maps, container pieces, and 1-Up Dolls (default: on)
- **randomize_pbag_locations** — Include P-Bags in the item shuffle (default: off)
- **randomize_key_locations** — Shuffle the six palace small keys, otherwise each key remains in its own palace (default: off)
- **randomize_allkey_location** — Shuffle the Magical Key instead of keeping it at its vanilla Great Palace location (default: off)
- **limit_obscure_locations** — Exclude a random selection of obscure checks from progression placement (default: off)
- **dark_room_difficulty** — Light rules: 0 = every dark room needs Candle/Fire, 1 = only the harder ones do, 2 = none do (0-2, default: 0)
- **hard_logic_tricks** — Allow precise alternatives such as itemless fire pillar crossings and upthrust-less block breaking (default: off)
- **item_location_hints** — Enable hint messages from marked NPCs and objects (default: on)
- **zelda_hint** — Zelda gives a free hint: none, Flute, Magical Key, or Jump location (default: none)
- **hint_giver_percent** — Percentage of hint givers that provide a useful hint (0-100, default: 25)

### Spell Options

- **shuffle_spells_among_wise_men** — Shuffle spells among Wise Men only (default: off)
- **randomize_spell_locations** — Put spells in the general item pool; overrides Wise Man shuffling (default: off)
- **randomize_spell_cost** — Randomize spell MP costs by -25% to +10% (default: on)

### Dungeon Options

- **randomize_dungeon_rooms** — Randomize dungeon room layouts (default: off)
- **randomize_dungeon_locations** — Shuffle all seven Quest 1 palaces among palace tiles
- **randomize_dungeon_boss** — Shuffle bosses among the six crystal palaces (default: off)
- **boss_item_locations** — Add a randomized AP check alongside each crystal boss reward (default: off)
- **randomize_town_locations** — Shuffle all eight Quest 1 towns among town tiles (default: off)
- **randomize_dungeon_tileset** — Shuffle original and custom palace graphics (default: off)

### Enemy Options

- **enemy_difficulty** — Difficulty rank of randomized enemies (1-4, default: 1)
- **enemy_randomization_method** — Vanilla, Random Spawns, or Random Types (default: Vanilla)
- **randomize_enemy_spawners** — Shuffle spawner locations and randomize drop-spawner enemies (default: off)
- **enemy_enigma** — Enigma transforms enemies into random enemies instead of Blue Slimes (default: off)
- **randomize_enemy_hp** — Randomize enemy HP by -25% to +25% (default: off)
- **randomize_enemy_damage** — Randomize enemy damage by -25% to +25% (default: off)

### Other Options

- **randomize_level_cost** — Randomize level-up costs by -25% to +25% (default: on)
- **randomize_xp** — Randomize enemy and P-Bag XP by -25% to +25% (default: on)
- **randomize_palette** — Randomize color palettes; palettes remain adjustable in-game (default: off)
- **force_quit_penalty** — Lose 75% XP from game-over warping; losing all lives still costs 75% when disabled (default: on)
- **starting_quest** — Quest 1 or Quest 2 (default: Quest 1)
- **starting_attack_level** — Starting Attack level; no logic effect (1-9, default: 1)
- **starting_magic_level** — Starting Magic level; no logic effect (1-9, default: 1)
- **starting_life_level** — Starting Life level; no logic effect (1-9, default: 1)
- **kakusu_required_count** — Gold Slimes needed for the bundled Kakusu reward (0-12, default: 7)
- **kakusu_individual_location_count** — Gold Slime kills exposed as individual AP checks (0-12, default: 0)
- **crystals_required_count** — Crystals required for Great Palace and the Quest 2 goal (0-6, default: 6)

The APWorld also provides **Vanilla-like** and **Full Randomizer** presets.

## Important Access Rules

### West and Death Mountain

- Rauru Pass and Rauru Cave require Glove or Bracelet.
- Jump Cave requires Jump and enough light for a moderate dark room.
- The Mido boulder requires Hammer; the Fire Cave route into Mido requires Fire.
- Saria Bridge requires Note, or Jump plus Fairy.
- The back route between Death Mountain and Saria2 requires enough light for the hardest dark rooms.
- The main Midoro Field route to Royal Cemetery requires Hammer.

### Raft and East

- The main crossing to East requires Raft.
- North Islands requires Raft, Hammer, and Jump or Fairy.
- Kasuto Area requires Flute, or Bracelet plus Boots.
- Valley of Death requires Cross.
- Maze Island requires Stab Down.
- Dragmire is a Quest 2 route requiring Cross, Boots, and Raft.

### Palace Tiles and Palace Content

When palace locations are shuffled, access tied to the overworld tile stays with the tile, while boss and internal requirements stay with the palace content.

Tile access requirements:

- Parapa tile: moderate dark-room access
- Midoro tile: no additional item requirement
- Island tile: Fairy, Jump, and moderate dark-room access
- Maze Island tile: Stab Down
- Palace on the Sea tile: Boots
- Three Eye Rock tile: Flute
- Great Palace tile: Cross, Jump or Fairy, hardest dark-room access, and access to Kasuto Area

Palace-clear requirements, in addition to reaching the occupied tile:

- Parapa: hardest dark-room access; 3 keys when keys are shuffled
- Midoro: Glove, hardest dark-room access; 4 keys when keys are shuffled
- Island: Glove and Stab Down; 4 keys when keys are shuffled
- Maze Island: Reflect; 6 keys when keys are shuffled
- Palace on the Sea: Fairy; 5 keys when keys are shuffled
- Three Eye Rock: Glove, Jump, Stab Down, and Fairy; 6 keys when keys are shuffled
- Great Palace content additionally requires the configured crystal count

The Magical Key satisfies every small-key threshold.

## Victory Conditions

- **Quest 1:** Complete the Great Palace ending.
- **Quest 2:** Defeat Ganon in Dragmire Tower. Logic also requires Bottle, Summon, Magical Key, Glove, Stab Down, and the configured crystal count.
