# Hacknet

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Randomization scatters several items across the multiworld, including, but not limited to: executables, RAM storage,
faction access, and shell amount limits. Almost every mission in Hacknet is a check, but you can modify your YAML to 
also include things such as admin access and PointClicker upgrades as checks, as well.

## What is the goal of Hacknet?

This is dependent on your YAML settings. Presently, there are five different goals to choose from:
* `Heartstopper` - The vanilla ending to the game.
* `Altitude Loss` - Reaching the end credits for the DLC, Hacknet Labyrinths.
* `Veteran` - Gaining admin access on a super secret and notoriously difficult node.
* `VIP` - Completing EVERY mission for both Entropy and CSEC. If Labyrinths content is enabled, this includes The Kaguya
 Trials.
* `Completionist` - Everything above.

## What Hacknet items can appear in other players' worlds?

* Executables - Crackers (e.g., `FTPBounce`), and other various executables (e.g., `ThemeChanger`, `NetmapOrganizer`)
* Executable Packs - Depending on your settings.
* Progressive RAM - When RAM limits are enabled in the YAML. Each item adds 50mb to your in-game RAM.
* Progressive Shell Limit - When shell limits are enabld in the YAML. You can never have more than this amount of shells
 open at once.
* Progressive Faction Access - The more you have, the more missions you can take on.
* PointClicker Items - Affect your PointClicker score and rate.
* Traps
    * ForkBomb - A classic, can be killed if you're fast enough.
    * Fake Connection - A fake connection banner with the stinger SFX to give you a heart attack.
    * Random Theme - Randomizes your in-game theme to one of the presets.
    * ETAS Trap - Toggleable in the YAML, activates the Emergency Trace Aversion Sequence.

## What are location checks in Hacknet?

* Missions - Almost every mission sends out a check when completed
* PointClicker Upgrades - Toggleable in the YAML, all 50 of the PointClicker upgrades can send out a check when 
purchased.
* Achievements - Toggleable in the YAML, every Steam achievement Hacknet has can send out a check. (This also works in 
the GOG release of Hacknet.)
* Admin Access - Toggleable in the YAML, every node that you can gain admin access on can also send out a check. When 
enabled, this adds ~140 new locations.
