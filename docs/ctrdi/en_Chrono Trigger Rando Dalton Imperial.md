# Chrono Trigger Rando-Dalton Imperial

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) or [the ctrando.com settings builder](https://ctrando.com/toml_form) contains all of the options you need to configure and export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Rando-Dalton Imperial is an open world randomizer for Chrono Trigger. At its core, it tries to maintain as much of the vanilla play experience as possible while offering a high degree of customization so that players can custom tailor their experience. It uses a highly configurable dynamic enemy scaling system to keep a smooth difficulty curve through the whole seed.

Rando-Dalton Imperial offers a high degree of customization as to what can be randomized, but includes:
 - Treasure chests
 - Event rewards
 - Tab locations
 - Enemy charms and drops
 - Techs and elements
 - Shops
 - Bosses and midbosses
 - Entrances
 - Time gates
 - Items from the DS version of the game
 - A special surprise hiding in the right telepod


## What items and locations get shuffled?

All items normally found in chests and event rewards, as well as character tech levels are shuffled into the multiworld pool. Key items can optionally be placed in shops, but will always be considered out of logic.

The randomized locations include chests, event/quest rewards and tab locations.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Any of the items which can be shuffled may also be placed into another player's world.

## What does another world's item look like in RDI?

Items belonging to other worlds will be noted in the treasure text when opening a chest or acquiring an item. The text will state what the treasure is and what player it is being sent to. In the cases where the item is visible in game, it will be represented by a blue sparkle.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

Items are silently delivered into the player's inventory. Keep an eye on the client window for new items!
