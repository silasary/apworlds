# Kirby Super Star Ultra

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure
and export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

### All Main Games and Sub-games
- Access to all main games and sub-games (if enabled) will be blocked until the game is received from the multiworld.
- Kirby will be unable to absorb copy abilities from enemies in all modes until the respective ability has been received from the multiworld.

### Dyna Blade
- Stage progression is locked by receiving `Progressive Stage` from the multiworld.
- Access to each of the EX stages is also locked by the multiworld.

### The Great Cave Offensive
- Access to each subsection of The Great Cave Offensive is blocked behind collecting a progressive amount of keys.
- If enabled, the doors will instead be accessible by collecting a certain amount of treasure, crossing a certain gold threshold.

### Milky Way Wishes
- Access to each planet within the galaxy is locked by receiving them from the multiworld. 
- Each player is given a single planet to begin with. This can be the hidden `???` planet.

## What is the goal of Kirby Super Star Ultra in Archipelago?
There are 6 total goals that can be selected in the options:

- In the Milky Way Wishes goal, the player must unlock the "Milky Way Wishes" game, met a certain condition to unlock 
Galactic Nova, and defeat Marx. This is the default option.

- In Main Game Completion goal, the player will clear a certain number of specified games.

- In The Arena goal, the player must unlock "The Arena", and achieve victory.

- In Revenge of the King goal, the player must unlock the game "Revenge of the King", and defeat Masked Dedede.

- In Meta Knightmare Ultra goal, the player must unlock "Meta Knightmare Ultra", and defeat Galacta Knight.

- In Marx Soul goal, the player must unlock the TRUE arena, and defeat Marx Soul.

## What is considered a location in Kirby Super Star Ultra?

- Completing each stage in Spring Breeze
- Completing each stage in Dyna Blade
- Collecting each treasure in The Great Cave Offensive
- Defeating each area boss in The Great Cave Offensive
- Beating Dedede in each round of Gourmet Race
- Completing each chapter in Revenge of Meta Knight
- Collecting an ability in Milky Way Wishes
- Defeating the boss on each planet of Milky Way Wishes
- Completing each stage in Revenge of the King
- Every round won in The Arena
- Defeating a boss / completing each level in Meta Knightmare Ultra
- Every round won in Helper to Hero
- Every round won in The True Arena

### Future Locations (Not Implemented Yet)
- Collecting 1-Ups, Maxim Tomatoes, and Static Foods (Optional)
- Interacting with Copy Essences (optional)

## Which items can be in another player's world?

- Each Main-Game (11 total)
- Each Sub-Game (5 total)
- Every Copy Ability (23 total)
- The Great Cave Offensive Treasures (60 total)
- Progressive Stages for Dyna Blade (4 Total)
- Progressive Keys for TGCO (minimum of 3 total)
- Progressive Levels for Meta Knightmare Ultra (4 total)
- Planets in Milky Way Wishes (7 total)
- Extra stages in Dyna Blade (2 total)
- 1-Ups, Foods, and Invincibility Candy (Filler)
