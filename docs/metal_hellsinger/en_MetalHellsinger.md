# Metal: Hellsinger

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file.

## I haven't played Metal: Hellsinger before

<u>**Play vanilla first.**</u> It is **_heavily discouraged_** to play this randomizer before playing the vanilla game.
It is recommended that you finish Sheol in the vanilla game before playing the randomizer.

## What does randomization do to this game?

In the Metal: Hellsinger Randomizer, every item in the game can be randomized. All Hells, Torments, Weapons, Songs, Outfits, Coat of Arms and Multiplier Pickups can be randomized, as well as player abilities like Jumping, Reloading, Dashing and destroying in-game destructibles.<br>

Ability shuffling is an option available from the options page to shuffle certain abilities
(Jumping, Reloading, Ultimates, Dashing, Slaughter and destroying in-game destructibles),
preventing them from being used until they are unlocked.<br>

Song and Outfit randomization and other options like additional weapon variants are also available and can be turned on in the client mod.

## What is the goal of Metal: Hellsinger when randomized?

The standard goal is the same as the vanilla game. Beat the Red Judge atop the mountain in Sheol.

Alternatively, a number of Aspects can be slain instead.

## What items from Metal: Hellsinger can appear in another player's world?

Every item has a chance to appear in another player's world.

## How many checks are in Metal: Hellsinger?

There are between 150 and 500 checks located across the world of Metal: Hellsinger.

## What do items from other worlds look like in Metal: Hellsinger?

No custom sprites/objects are currently implemented to show out of world items.

## Is there a tracker pack?

There is currently no visual tracker pack for this randomizer.

You can use the Universal Tracker (by Faris and qwint) to find a complete list of what checks are in logic with your current items. You can find it on the Archipelago Discord. This tracker is an extension of the regular Archipelago Text Client.

## What should I know regarding logic?

In general:
- You always start with one of the eight Hells.
- You are expected to be able to beat any Hell if you do not randomize any ability.
- In case of randomizing abilities, there are some soft requirements the logic abides to. For details check the Archipelago Discord thread

## Does this game have item and location groups?

Yes! To find what they are, open up the Archipelago Text Client while connected to a Metal: Hellsinger session and type in `/item_groups` or `/location_groups`.

## Is there anything else I should know?

- Death Link can be toggled in game, and it can be set to receive traps instead of deaths.
