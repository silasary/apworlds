# N++

## What is this game?

N++ is the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, or 6th game in the N franchise depending on who you ask. Created by Metanet Software, 
the N franchise started as a flash game, saw multiple console releases in N+, and the franchise recieved it's ultimate 
game in N++. The goal of each level is simple, reach the door switch then reach the door before time expires, collecting 
gold along the way to extend your time.

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

The original game is a race against time, but as speedrunner became faster, the time became irrelevant, so that's what 
this randomizer randomizes. The time.

The time at the beginning of each level, the time from collecting gold, and the maximum time possible is reduced, 
requiring regaining them through items, as well as unlock progressive levels in episodes to play them and send items.

## How do I play it?

Each level gets unlocked individually, so navigation through level view is required to play levels and complete them.
Eventually, the episode will become available to play in episode view. Completing episodes contributes to the victory 
condition.

## What items get shuffled?

Each episode has 6 (or 5 if the first level starts unlocked) "Progressive Episode Unlocks" which unlock each level in an
episode, with the 6th unlocking the ability to play the episode in episode view.

In addition to the level unlocks, the following items in varying increments are also added to the pool based on your 
randomization to increase time to eventually allow all challenges and episodes to be completed:
- +1 Second of Starting Time
- +2 Seconds of Starting Time
- +5 Seconds of Starting Time
- +10 Seconds of Starting Time
- +1 Second of Maximum Time
- +2 Seconds of Maximum Time
- +5 Seconds of Maximum Time
- +10 Seconds of Maximum Time
- +1 frame of Gold Time
- +2 frames of Gold Time
- +5 frames of Gold Time
- +10 Frames of Gold Time

## What locations get shuffled?

Each level completion, episode completion, and challenge completion is a location. The challenges for each level are
randomized as well, so it may not be all of the original challenges, or it even may include challenges that aren't in
the original game.
