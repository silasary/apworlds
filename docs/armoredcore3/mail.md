# Mails in the Armored Core 3 Randomizer

In Armored Core 3 you get mails from clients and other pilots while you play.
With the **Mailsanity** option turned on, every mail you receive counts as a **check**: the moment you read the mail, a randomized item is sent out for it.

## The Mailsanity option

| Setting | What happens |
|---|---|
| **None** | Mails do nothing. |
| **Standard** | Only the mails that arrive through normal play are checks (**42 mails**). |
| **Include Challenges** | The standard mails plus mails that need something special in a mission (**60 mails** in total). |

17 more mails exist in the game, but they are **not included yet** (see the last section).

### The OP-INTENSIFY mails

These arrive when you complete a mission with the optional part **OP-INTENSIFY** equipped.

| Mail | What you need to do |
|---|---|
| OP-INTENSIFY - Various Sensors | Complete **Eliminate Intruders** and defeat the enemy AC |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Blade Wave | Complete **Destroy Massive Weapon** |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Enhanced Cooling | Complete **Destroy Massive MT** |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Cannon Operation | Complete **Destroy Massive MT** and destroy **both parts** of the MT  |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Turning | Complete **Bomb Disarmament** and defeat the enemy AC |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Missile Interception | Complete **Defend Water Processors** and defeat the enemy AC |
| OP-INTENSIFY - Improved Targeting | Complete **Defend Ruglen Laboratory** and defeat the enemy AC |

All of these have to be done with OP-INTENSIFY equipped on your AC.

- **Shopsanity ON:** parts are items in the multiworld. You need to have **received the OP-INTENSIFY part** first, and the randomizer makes sure it is obtainable before expecting these mails.
- **Shopsanity OFF:** Those won't be included
