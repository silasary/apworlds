# Armored Core 3 Archipelago

## What does randomization do to this game?
Mission unlocks and Parts are shuffled into the multiworld item pool. 

## What is the goal of Armored Core 3 when randomized?
- In Progressive Missions mode, complete Infiltrate Layered Hub. 
- In Mission-Sanity mode, complete a number of missions you can set between 1 and 49.
## What items and locations get shuffled?

Each mission completed is a location where you can find a check.
With Mailsanity turned on each piece of Mail read is a location.
With Shopsanity on, each part listing can be purchased for items separate from the ones you already own.

## Which items can be in another player's world?
Missions or Progressive Missions depending on your setting, as well as Parts and Credits as Filler.

## What does another world's item look like in Armored Core 3
Your item acquisitions take the form of having completed a mission or buying something from the shop.
You'll have to check the Armored Core 3 client to know the actual items.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
The client will update the Mission list with what you have access to. If you are currently in the Mission list when sent a mission, you have to exit and re-enter for it to update.
Also, your inventory will be updated if you receive parts, you may need to renter the Assembly menu. 
## Notes and Limitations
- The mission menu restructuring, shop description and Optional Part name edit code requires a connection to the client and a connection to the server.
