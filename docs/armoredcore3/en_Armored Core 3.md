# Armored Core 3 Archipelago

## What does randomization do to this game?
Mission unlocks are shuffled into the multiworld item pool. 

## What is the goal of Armored Core 3 when randomized?
- In Progressive Missions mode, complete Infiltrate Layered Hub. 
- In Mission-Sanity mode, complete a number of missions you can set between 1 and 49.

## Which items can be in another player's world?
Missions or Progressive Missions depending on your goal, as well as filler (Credits).

## What does another world's item look like in Armored Core 3
Your item acquisitions take the form of having completed a mission.
You'll have to check the Armored Core 3 client to know the actual items.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
The client will update the Mission list with what you have access to. If you are currently in the Mission list when sent a mission, you have to exit and re-enter for it to update.

## Notes and Limitations
- The mission menu restructuring code require a connection to the client and a connection to the server.
