# Celeste Classic

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for Celeste Classic](../player-options)
contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file.

## What is the goal of Celeste Classic when randomized?

Reach the summit and touch the flag after collecting a certain number of strawberries.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Various level objects (as well as the double dash) are shuffled into the item pool. Receiving them allows the corresponding objects to be used.

Additionally, checkpoints are added to the item pool. There is one for every interval of 500M, as well as one for the Old Site and one for 2200M (the room with the double dash chest). The 100M "checkpoint" is always unlocked. Warping to a checkpoint is done through the PICO-8 pause menu: While hovering over the "GOTO" option, press left and right to cycle between your unlocked checkpoints, and press either the jump or dash button to warp there.

## What is considered a location check in Celeste Classic?

Entering new rooms for the first time sends a location, and strawberries are locations.

## What does another world's item look like in Celeste Classic?

Currently, items are not listed until they are sent. This may change in the future.
