# Jet Set Radio Future

## What does randomization do to this game?

You play the city as it is after the story: the free-skate chapter, with the Garage as your hub. Each area stays locked
until its Unlock item arrives. You start with one area (Dogenzaka Hill unless you choose another) and one character
(Beat unless you choose another). Everything else you need comes from the multiworld.

If you skate into an area you don't have yet, Corn stops you and the client sends you back to the Garage.

## What counts as a check?

| Check | How many | Option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graffiti Souls lying in the levels | 40 | always on |
| Graffiti Souls from Street Challenges | 65 | Include Street Challenges |
| Mystery Tapes | 13 | Include Mystery Tapes |
| Beating a rival gang: Doom Riders, Love Shockers, Immortals | 3 | Include Rival Gang Battles |
| Recruiting Rhyth, Boogie, Cube, Jazz and Soda | 5 | Character Unlock Checks: Recruits |
| The characters Roboy's Test Runs hand out | 12 more | Character Unlock Checks: All |
| A Jet rank on each of Roboy's Test Runs | 39 | Include Jet Ranks |
| Graffiti tag spots, save point tags included | 572 | Include Graffiti Tags (off by default) |

## Which items are in the pool?

- An Unlock item for every area except your starting one.
- Every character except the one you start with. Characters never block progress.
- Mystery Tape items, one per area (with Include Mystery Tapes on). An area's Street Challenges only open once you have
  its Mystery Tape item, your starting area's included.
- Graffiti Soul items, which count toward Gouji's Tower. These are items, separate from the souls you pick up in the
  levels.
- Gouji's Tower Unlock.
- Filler: Spray Can Refill and Health Refill, which top up your cans or health the moment they arrive.

## What is the goal?

The Goal option has two choices.

**Defeat Gouji.** Gouji's Tower opens once you have the Gouji's Tower Unlock and enough Graffiti Soul items (Tower:
Graffiti Souls Needed). You can also require sprayed tag spots and cleared Street Challenges. When the Tower is open,
talk to Corn in the Garage and say Yes. Beat the final boss to finish.

**Jet Ranks.** Earn a Jet rank on as many Test Runs as Jet Ranks Needed asks for. As in the normal game, Roboy in the
Garage offers an area's three Test Runs once you hold every Graffiti Soul of that area, Street Challenge souls included.
In a Jet Ranks seed Gouji's Tower is optional.

## What does another world's item look like in this game?

Items have no shape in the game itself. When you collect a check, the client window shows what you found and who it
belongs to. Items you receive appear as a notice over the game screen.
